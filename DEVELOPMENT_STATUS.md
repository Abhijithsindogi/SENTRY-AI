# SENTRY AI development status

## Phase 1 · Calibration and caregiver workflow

ESP32 calibration profiles, model inputs, latest assessments, and assessment history persist in SQLite. Profiles are keyed by patient, source, mode, activity context, and feature set. Isolation Forest rebuilds from normalized data. Fresh, calibrated persistent deviations create caregiver check-ins in the assessment transaction. Alerts have episode deduplication, a 10-minute cooldown, read and acknowledgement state, and review notes.

The main dashboard includes calibration progress, a caregiver overview, distinct live and simulation unread counts, and a guided simulation that fits a synthetic baseline and evaluates a persistent deviation. Reset removes simulation check-ins and the guided scenario's AI state. Existing health records and application files were preserved. Source snapshots are in `.backups/continuation` and `.backups/phase-two`.

## Phase 2 · Acquisition and history

Serial and BLE samples persist in SQLite with timezone, freshness, ordering, and duplicate checks. Live sample retention defaults to 30 days and is applied at ingestion. The browser connection center scans, selects a device, displays connection and freshness, and reads optional battery data when supported. The managed connection retries discovery after a disconnect. The original CLI remains available.

Date filters and day/week/month grouping drive accessible source-specific charts, a data table, and PDF/CSV reports. The importer validates CSV and Da Halo ZIP uploads and deduplicates records. Overlaps with the original archive are deduplicated in analytics. Vendor dates and unknown units are preserved. The legacy Sekyo view and replay keep using the original imported JSON.

## Phase 3 · Roles, notifications and startup

Loopback-only access and optional environment-configured patient/caregiver credentials are implemented for a single local pair. The app stores profiles, emergency contact information, and reduced-motion settings. Patients can manage imports and devices. Caregiver notes are restricted to caregiver/shared access. Telegram delivery is opt-in, disabled until configured, and uses a generic message. The app no longer fetches fonts from third-party sites.

The Windows BAT launcher starts the backend and available ESP32 bridge. It optionally starts a selected CLI BLE bridge, or uses browser-managed BLE. It refuses an occupied backend port and shuts down its own processes. The PowerShell script remains available.
