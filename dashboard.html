            Project information

            A local healthcare engineering prototype for caregivers and patients.

            Problem
            Wearable measurements and family observations are fragmented. Caregivers need usable context before deciding whether to check in.
            Proposed solution
            One dashboard turns device evidence and patient diary entries into a review workflow, with optional family follow-up.
            Innovations
            Adaptive signal selection, device-matched personal references, explicit anomaly evidence and consent-aware family response.
            Technology stack
            ESP32, serial and standard Bluetooth LE, Python, FastAPI, SQLite, scikit-learn, and a local web dashboard.
            Social impact
            Designed to reduce the effort of coordinating care and make data gaps visible. Outcome improvement is unmeasured.
            Scalability and future scope
            Expand device adapters and multilingual reporting. Validate signal quality and accessibility with participants before any wider rollout.
